\section{Functional Requirements}
\label{sec:functional_requirements}

The functional requirements (FRs) define the specific capabilities, user actions, system responses, and operational workflows implemented within the UniExpert platform.

\textbf{Stakeholder} & Student Project Lead, Faculty Advisor, Industry Mentor, System Administrator \\ \hline

\section{Non-Functional Requirements}
\label{sec:non_functional_requirements}

The non-functional requirements (NFRs) specify the qualitative constraints, performance thresholds, security baselines, and architectural standards enforced across the system.

\subsection{Security}
\label{subsec:nfr_security}

\begin{table}[H]
\centering
\small
\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{|l|X|}
\hline
\rowcolor{lightgray!40} \textbf{NFR01} & \textbf{Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)} \\ \hline
\textbf{Description} & The system SHALL strictly enforce authorization boundaries across all 6 roles, verifying JWT bearer tokens and claims on every backend API invocation. \\ \hline
\textbf{Stakeholder} & Student Project Lead, Faculty Advisor, Industry Mentor, Department Coordinator, System Administrator, Institutional Auditor \\ \hline
\end{tabularx}
\end{table}

\begin{table}[H]
\centering
\small
\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{|l|X|}
\hline
\rowcolor{lightgray!40} \textbf{NFR02} & \textbf{Token-Based Session Security \& Cryptographic Expiry} \\ \hline
\textbf{Description} & Access tokens SHALL expire after 15 minutes of inactivity; refresh tokens SHALL be stored exclusively in secure, HttpOnly, SameSite cookies with a 7-day maximum lifespan. \\ \hline
\textbf{Stakeholder} & All Stakeholders \\ \hline
\end{tabularx}
\end{table}

\begin{table}[H]
\centering
\small
\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{|l|X|}
\hline
\rowcolor{lightgray!40} \textbf{NFR03} & \textbf{Password Hashing \& Brute-Force Lockout} \\ \hline
\textbf{Description} & User passwords SHALL be hashed using Argon2id with unique cryptographic salts. Accounts SHALL be temporarily locked for 15 minutes after 5 consecutive failed login attempts. \\ \hline
\textbf{Stakeholder} & All Stakeholders \\ \hline
\end{tabularx}
\end{table}

\begin{table}[H]
\centering
\small
\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{|l|X|}
\hline
\rowcolor{lightgray!40} \textbf{NFR04} & \textbf{Transport and Storage Cryptography} \\ \hline
\textbf{Description} & All communication across client and microservice boundaries SHALL mandate TLS 1.3 encryption. Sensitive database fields (tokens, CV attachments, private contact numbers) SHALL use AES-256-GCM. \\ \hline
\textbf{Stakeholder} & System Administrator \\ \hline
\end{tabularx}
\end{table}
